# Discrete Mathematics · Interactive

**Logic, sets, graphs, counting — foundations of CS**

**Status:** Planned

Part of [Hengzhe's Science Classroom](https://hengzhe-science-classroom.github.io/hub/) · Tier 3 · Undergraduate

## What You'll Learn

- Propositional and predicate logic
- Sets, relations, and functions
- Counting: permutations, combinations, pigeonhole
- Graph theory: paths, trees, coloring
- Recurrences and generating functions
- Number theory and cryptography basics

## References

- Kenneth H. Rosen, *Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications* (8th ed., McGraw-Hill)
- Lehman, Leighton & Meyer, *Mathematics for Computer Science* (MIT OpenCourseWare)
- Lovász, Pelikán & Vesztergombi, *Discrete Mathematics*

## Features (when built)

- **Interactive visualizations** — every concept comes alive with draggable, animated demos
- **KaTeX math rendering** — rigorous notation throughout
- **Exercises with hints & solutions** — from computational to proof-based
- **Progress tracking** — saved locally in your browser
- **Zero dependencies** — just open the link and learn
